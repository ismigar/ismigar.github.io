The problem Gnosi solves

Your research is a workflow, not a pile of apps.

References in one tool, project tables in another, notes somewhere else and the final text in a fourth place. The friction is not capturing more information; it is preserving the path from claim back to evidence.

↔

Repeated work

The same resource and metadata copied between a reference manager, notes and project tracker.

⌁

Evidence without context

Highlights and ideas become detached from the page, paragraph or source that supports them.

□

Knowledge on borrowed land

A pricing or policy change can put years of structured work behind somebody else’s rules.

Gnosi’s answer

One open chain of custody for knowledge.

Sources, evidence, notes, links, project views and citations remain connected while the underlying files stay readable outside Gnosi.

The golden path

A complete research cycle in four steps

Start with your own material. AI can assist, but the workflow does not depend on it.

01

Capture or import

Bring in references by DOI, ISBN, arXiv, PMID, BibTeX or RIS. Save web pages and open PDFs or EPUBs inside the workspace.

Zotero-compatible capture · Web Clipper
02

Read with evidence

Annotate a source and keep quotations tied to page, paragraph, chapter or timestamp so every claim can be checked.

PDF/EPUB reader · Provenance
03

Connect and structure

Turn reading notes into your own synthesis. Use wikilinks, the graph, databases, boards, timelines and research milestones.

Markdown · Graph · Database views
04

Write and cite

Insert active citations from Gnosi into Word or LibreOffice and generate the bibliography without duplicating the library.

CSL/citeproc · Word · LibreOffice
Built around the work

A

Reference management, rich readers, annotations, atomic reading notes and exact-source highlighting share the same context.

Sovereignty by design

Your workspace must outlive the application.

Gnosi uses regular files as the source of truth and rebuildable indexes for speed. You can inspect, back up, version and migrate the knowledge without asking permission.

  • Markdown + YAMLReadable with a text editor and compatible with ordinary file tools.
  • Local-firstPersonal use does not require a Gnosi cloud account.
  • AGPL-3.0The application is free software and its development is public.

A personal tool, shared openly

Gnosi was built for a real research workflow.

It began as a way to stop duplicating knowledge across Notion, Obsidian and Mendeley while keeping the advantages of each. It is shared with the community as free software, without pretending to be a finished company or a universal solution.
